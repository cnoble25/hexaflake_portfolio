# Early Game Projects

*Unity prototypes from high school, 2024*

Smaller games where I learned Unity and C#.

* **[Movement Game](https://github.com/cnoble25/Movement-Game):** a 3D movement game built around wall-running and wall-jumping. Solo.
* **[Defender](https://github.com/cnoble25/Oceanus-Real):** an arcade shooter in the style of *Defender*, where you pilot a submarine, fight sharks and jellyfish, and rescue civilians. Team project.
* **Morning Run:** a 3D platformer prototype with a player state machine, built in two days. Solo.
* **Spelunky 3:** a 2D platformer prototype with items, an NPC and enemies, made with friends.

### Tech
* Unity, C#

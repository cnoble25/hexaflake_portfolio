# Bon Bunny

*Team of 5, Unity, 2024*

A 24-level game where you steer a balloon bunny through the sky, built with control schemes for the Xbox Adaptive Controller to help teach fine motor control to children with disabilities.

### The game
* Thrust-and-torque balloon flight across 24 levels, with hazards like birds, satellites, thunderclouds and wind funnels.
* Input schemes for the Xbox Adaptive Controller, alongside gamepad, keyboard and joystick.

### The team
I was one of two programmers, with Nikolay Sitnikov. Liana Jimenez produced and did art, Zoe Gaston did art and James Hon did sound.

### Tech

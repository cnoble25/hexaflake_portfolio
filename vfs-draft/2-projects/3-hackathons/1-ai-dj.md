# AI DJ

*1st place of 44 AI teams, WashU Hackathon (Skandalaris × Mayfield), Oct 2025*

An agent that builds a playlist from a natural-language description of the vibe you want, then mixes from one track to the next.

### What we built
* Won 1st of 44 AI teams, out of 200+ participants.
* Playlists are grounded by cosine-similarity search over an embedded vector database of songs.
* Beat-matched transitions between tracks.

### Tech
* Python, Flask
* Vector search, embeddings
* librosa
